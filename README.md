# 🏥 MediKiosk

### AI-Powered Multilingual Clinical History Intake Platform

MediKiosk is an AI-powered healthcare intake platform designed to help patients record their clinical history through **voice or touch**, digitize previous medical documents, and generate a **structured, physician-ready summary** before consultation.

It is designed especially for environments where patients may face barriers related to literacy, language, accessibility, or limited consultation time.

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional patient history-taking can be:

- Time-consuming for healthcare professionals
- Difficult for patients with low literacy
- Challenging for elderly or accessibility-needing patients
- Dependent on language and communication barriers
- Inefficient when previous medical records are available only as physical documents
- Prone to incomplete or unstructured patient information

Doctors often need to spend valuable consultation time collecting information that could have been captured before the consultation.
